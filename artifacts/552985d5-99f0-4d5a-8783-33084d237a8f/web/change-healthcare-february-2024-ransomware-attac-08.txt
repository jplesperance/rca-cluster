## Compromised Credentials and Absent MFA Lead to Change Healthcare Data Breach

In February, attackers linked to the BlackCat/ALPHV ransomware exploited minimal security barriers at Change Healthcare. During the congressional testimony, it was disclosed that the attackers utilized stolen credentials to gain entry into Change’s Citrix framework, likely sourced from an initial access broker with no multifactor authentication (MFA). [...] American Data Security: The cyberattack at Change Healthcare highlighted the need for robust protection of sensitive health data. Since the attack, Energy and Commerce Republicans have been investigating the incident to understand its causes, prevention strategies, and ways to ensure continuous care access for Americans.
 The Magnitude of the Breach: Change Healthcare, a major player in health payment processing, handles 15 billion medical claims annually, representing nearly 40% of all U.S. claims. The February cyberattack disrupted services, causing significant payment backlogs and severe cash flow issues in healthcare facilities, thus endangering patient care.
